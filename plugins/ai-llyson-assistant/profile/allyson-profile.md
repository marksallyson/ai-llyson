## Career Landscape Clarity (from coaching session)

**Data Science vs Decision Science:**
- *Data Science* = broad umbrella: modeling, statistics, prediction, ML
- *Decision Science* (Ibotta's title) = flavor of DS focused on using data to drive decisions — experimentation, causal inference, A/B testing, behavioral analysis
- The two overlap. Decision Scientists at Ibotta do predictive modeling too. The title doesn't box her out of it.
- Her profile fits squarely in the Decision Science world. The modeling she wants (survival analysis, prediction) is the *next level* of this job, not a different job.

**Is DS dying?** No — but the umbrella is fragmenting into:
- Applied Scientist / ML Engineer (models, infra)
- Decision Scientist / Product Analyst (insight, strategy, experimentation) ← her lane
- Analytics Engineer (data foundations) ← not her lane
