# Rommil Santiago

## At a Glance
Rommil Santiago is the founder of Experiment Nation, an 11,000+ member community for experimentation practitioners, and author of *Prove It or Lose It* (2025). He hosts a newsletter and runs practitioner interviews that cover the full range of roles — DS, PM, engineer, designer — who participate in experimentation programs.

## Why They Matter
Santiago's contribution is community and practitioner-level knowledge synthesis. While Kohavi and Deng write for statisticians, Santiago interviews practitioners at 50-person startups and 50,000-person enterprises and extracts the patterns. His work is particularly useful for understanding how experimentation programs actually function in practice — including the organizational failures, the political dynamics, and the skills gaps — versus how they're described in academic papers.

## Key Contributions
- **Experiment Nation community**: 11k+ member Slack/Discord for experimentation practitioners; newsletter, job board, and practitioner interviews
- **Practitioner interview series**: video and podcast interviews with practitioners from diverse company types; covers CRO, product experimentation, data science, and engineering perspectives
- **"Prove It or Lose It"** (2025): book aimed at practitioners making the case for experimentation in their organizations; covers stakeholder communication, experiment prioritization, and culture change
- **Newsletter**: regular posts on experimentation practice, career development, and community findings
- **Conference and meetup organizing**: runs the annual Experiment Nation conference

## Takeaways for Practice
- **Subscribe to the Experiment Nation newsletter for ongoing community-sourced knowledge.** It's practitioner-to-practitioner, not vendor marketing. You'll hear about failure modes and workarounds that don't make it into academic papers.
- **"Prove It or Lose It" is worth reading for the stakeholder communication chapters**, not the statistics chapters. If you're trying to build the case for more experimentation investment at Ibotta, Santiago's framing is more persuasive to business stakeholders than Kohavi's statistical arguments.
- **The practitioner interview series is useful for career benchmarking.** Watching interviews with DS leaders at comparable companies gives you a realistic sense of what "mature experimentation culture" looks like at a company Ibotta's size.
- **Experiment Nation Slack is worth joining** for real-time practitioner Q&A. If you have a specific methodological question about a promotions experiment, someone in the community has likely faced the same problem.
