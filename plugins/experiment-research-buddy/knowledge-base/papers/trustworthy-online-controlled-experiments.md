# Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments: A Practical Guide to A/B Testing

## At a Glance
Kohavi, R., Tang, D., & Xu, Y. (Cambridge University Press, 2020). THE definitive practitioner textbook on A/B testing — 400+ pages from the authors who built experimentation at Microsoft, Google, and LinkedIn. Covers experiment design, metric design, pitfalls, SRM, novelty effects, long-term effects, and organizational maturity.

## Why They Matter
This is the single most authoritative reference in the field. Any question about experiment methodology that isn't answered by a specific paper is answered here. The authors have collectively run more A/B tests than almost anyone alive and the book is ruthlessly practical — it's not academic theory, it's distilled production experience.

## Key Contributions
- Formalizes the OEC (Overall Evaluation Criterion) and how to design one that is sensitive, causal, and not gameable
- Comprehensive treatment of SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch) as a critical validity check
- Chapter on two-sided markets and marketplace interference — directly applicable to Ibotta
- "Surprising A/B Test Results" chapter: empirical evidence that expert intuition is wrong in ~30% of tested cases
- CUPED explanation and worked examples
- Novelty and primacy effect detection and correction
- Organizational maturity framework (how to build a testing culture)

## Takeaways for Practice
1. **Read Chapter 3 (OEC design) before every new experiment program.** The OEC framework is the most important strategic decision in any experiment.
2. **Chapter 5 (sample size and power)** has the clearest practical treatment of power analysis for practitioners — reference it when stakeholders push back on experiment duration.
3. **Chapter 7 (pitfalls)** is the most immediately useful chapter — it's a checklist of 30+ ways experiments go wrong. Walk through it before launch.
4. **Chapter 19 (two-sided markets)** is directly applicable to Ibotta's brand/consumer marketplace structure. Required reading for offer experiment design.
5. **Buy it.** It's $40 and worth 10x that in avoided mistakes.
