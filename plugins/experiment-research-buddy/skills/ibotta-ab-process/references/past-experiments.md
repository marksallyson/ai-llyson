## Why This Matters

- **Don't re-test the same thing.** Several experiments here have direct repeats or close variants
  (e.g., two Contextual Marketing Bonuses tests, multiple onboarding tests, multiple hotbar tests).
  Check this list before scoping a new experiment.
- **Calibrate your power analysis.** Historical effect sizes from similar experiments are your
  best prior for what realistic lifts look like at Ibotta. Before running a power analysis, pull
  the relevant past readout and use the control rate as your baseline.
- **Understand the readout format.** All Ibotta experiment readouts follow a similar slide
  structure. Reviewing a few of these before your first readout will help you match expectations.
- **Surface area coverage.** You can see which product surfaces have been heavily tested
  (onboarding, home screen, hotbar, retailer gallery) vs. less tested (affiliate offers, DPL comms).
